      How to use this dashboard

      Everything on this page is driven by one list of inventory lines. Change the list and every number above it recalculates immediately — there is nothing to save, refresh or submit.

      1. Start with the four KPIs

        They answer, in order: how much the stock is worth, how much of it is healthy, how many lines need a decision, and what fixing them costs.

        They cover the whole catalogue, not the filtered view, so the headline figures stay steady while you search.

      2. Read the two panels

        The status mix bar shows the split between in-stock, low and out-of-stock lines. The value by warehouse bars show where the money is concentrated.

      3. Find a line

        Type in the search box to match on SKU, product name or supplier — results narrow as you type. Use the Category dropdown to restrict to one product family.

        The count on the right of the toolbar says how many lines survived the filter.

      4. Sort any column

        Click a column heading to sort by it; click the same heading again to reverse. Keyboard users can tab to a heading and press Enter.

        Each column sorts by its own type — numbers numerically, dates chronologically, and Status by urgency (out of stock first) rather than alphabetically.

      5. Read the status pills

        Out of Stock means quantity is zero. Low Stock means quantity has fallen to or below the reorder level. In Stock is everything above it.

        Status is recalculated on every render from quantity and reorder level, so it can never disagree with the numbers beside it.

      6. Add a line

        Fill in the form on the right and press Add Record. SKUs must be unique; quantity and reorder level must be whole numbers; unit cost may carry decimals.

        If something is wrong, every problem field is flagged at once and the first is focused. The new row flashes briefly so you can see where it landed.

      7. Remove a line

        Press Delete at the end of a row. There is no confirmation dialog — the removal is announced in the strip beneath the table instead.

        Deleted a row by mistake? Reload the page and the original data set comes straight back.

      8. Know what does not persist

        This is a demonstration front end. Nothing is written to a server, a database or browser storage, and closing the tab discards every change you made.

      How each KPI is calculated

      KPI Definition Why it matters
      Inventory value Sum of quantity × unit cost across all lines. Working capital tied up in stock.
      Stock availability Lines holding more than their reorder level, as a percentage of all lines. A service-level proxy — how much of the catalogue can be fulfilled without expediting.
      Lines needing action Count of lines at or below reorder level, split into out of stock and low. The size of the purchasing workload today.
      Replenishment cost Sum of (reorder level − quantity) × unit cost, over flagged lines only. The budget needed to clear the shortfall.
